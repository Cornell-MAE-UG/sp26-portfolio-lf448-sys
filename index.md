## About Me

My name is Leah Fromm, and I am a sophomore studying biological engineering at Cornell University. My interests lie in agricultural engineering, specifically I hope to focus on biofuel technology and food insecurity. My previous experience has invovled hydroponic plant research, specifically when plants were innoculated with bacterial endophytes to inhibit auxin production. I was lucky enough to present said research at various fairs, earning fourth place at the Regeneron International Science and Engineering Fair. Since coming to Cornell, I have joined the project team Engineers for a Sustainable World, as a member of the Renewable Enegy Solutions division.
